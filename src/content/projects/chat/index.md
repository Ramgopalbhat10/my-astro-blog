Personal AI chat application. Replies stream in, and only allowed GitHub accounts can sign in.
- Streaming replies, with Mermaid diagrams rendered in the conversation.
- Web search you can turn on, with a count of the sources used on the answer.
- Attach images and PDFs to a message.
- Share a conversation or a single response.
